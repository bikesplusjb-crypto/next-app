## 1. Product purpose

ZigZag Mind is a mobile-first self-help web app for adults having a hard moment (anxiety, spiraling thoughts, low mood, urges to use, feeling alone, feeling unsafe). Core loop: FEEL → ACT → CHECK → LEARN → NEXT ACTION. It offers one small, concrete step, asks whether it helped, and points toward people.

## 2. Scope of this review

The `build` branch at the commit above: everything a person can see and every safety-relevant behavior. Not in scope: features on hold and not in the app (Snuggle Zags, Zags listens, Safe place, the Stage 7 new experiences, the Home redesign).

## 3. What it is

Self-help, non-clinical, non-diagnostic. Product safety logic: a crisis escalation flow and a human-support pathway (trusted people, Warm Line, 988, 911). Data stays on the device. A scripted character (Zags) guides breathing and grounding.

## 4. What it is not

Not therapy, medical care, diagnosis, a clinical risk assessment, or an emergency service. Its safety logic is **not clinically validated**: the phrase lists and thresholds are product heuristics. Zags is not AI, not a person, not a therapist, and not a replacement for human support.

## 5. Safety architecture

Three levels per visit (GREEN, YELLOW, RED), held in memory only. RED opens the crisis experience immediately and blocks every other flow until the person leaves it through the danger question or "That's not what I meant". Leaving RED always lands on YELLOW, never GREEN, in the same visit. A reload starts at GREEN. Full table: SAFETY_AUDIT §3.

## 6. RED / YELLOW / GREEN logic

- **RED (36 phrases: 23 audited + 13 OWNER-APPROVED INTERIM, incl. harm to others)** and **YELLOW (10 phrases)**: lists in REVIEW.md §2. Matching is whole-phrase after lowercasing and removing punctuation and apostrophes; RED wins over YELLOW. No negation, context, spelling-variant or language handling.
- **Automatic YELLOW:** 2× "I still feel bad", 2× a game answered "No", or 2× a 9–10 rating in one visit.
- **Checked text:** anxious "three things", Thought Parking, spiral "own step", "Thank you for ___". **My Plan is never checked** (by design).
- **Results for 60 inputs**, including the owner's confirmed misses: SAFETY_TEST_MATRIX.md.
- **Owner's note: a missed crisis is more dangerous than a false alarm, because the crisis screen is safe to show.**

## 7. Crisis flow

Help, "I don't feel safe", "Get help now", RED text, or Zags "Worse" → "I'm glad you told me." with Call/Text 988 and the danger question: **"Are you in danger of hurting yourself or someone else right now?"**

- **Yes / I'm not sure** → full crisis screen (911, 988, chat, trusted person, places with people, "Open my plan", the distance note).
- **No** → YELLOW → "What would help right now?" (talk to someone, open my plan, something grounding) → "Do you feel safer than a few minutes ago?" (Yes → Home at YELLOW; No / Not sure → full crisis screen). Calling or texting from here moves to that question in the same tap.
- **"That's not what I meant — go back"**: on the first crisis screen it returns Home at YELLOW. On the full crisis screen (after Yes / I'm not sure) it now asks "Do you feel safer?" (**OWNER-APPROVED INTERIM**, pending clinician). Decision D4.

## 8. Safety plan

My Plan: warning signs, things that help, places, trusted people (up to 3), professional, **time and distance plan** (three questions in the person's own words; no examples of means), things to avoid, reminders, plus "Set up with your people" (code word, check-in reminders). "I need my plan" shows the essentials read-only. Stored only on the phone, unencrypted, exportable, deletable. Plan text is never safety-checked.

Also: Zags sessions do not count toward Human First.

## 10. Human First

After 2 "Something else" taps or 2 finished steps without "That helped", once per visit: **"Would talking to a person help more than another answer?"** (Call someone / Text someone / Be around people / Not right now). Session only, never saved, never over a crisis screen.

## 11. Substance-use flow

"I have an urge to use (drink or drugs)" → a 15-minute waiting-it-out timer with a checklist, "ride the wave", a focus game, a check-in, and "I used". Safety copy (exact):

- "Already used, or took too much? Call 911."
- "Took too much, or feel unwell? Call 911." (after "I used")
- "Shaking, sweating, confused, or seeing things? Withdrawal can be a medical emergency. Get medical help."
- "Call or text 988 for support." (only calls: factual bug F2)

Questions: does "Already used… Call 911" over-escalate ordinary use; are "used" and "overdose" clearly distinguished; should Poison Control or SAMHSA's helpline appear; does it work across substances?

## 12. Supporter guide

A static page at zigzagmind.com/support for the people in someone's plan: what a code word means, what to say (ask directly about suicide), getting help together (988, 911), time and distance (no specifics), check-in reminders, and looking after yourself. Question: **"…keep trying, then go to them if you can."** Owner's candidate (not applied): "If it's safe for you to, go to them, or ask someone nearby to check on them. If they may be in immediate danger, call 911."

## 13. Privacy

All data stays in the browser on the phone (unencrypted). The app makes no network requests. Person-initiated sends (texts, share, maps, external links) do leave the phone. Since F7, external links no longer send the app's address. Since F3, nothing at all is saved from crisis screens or while RED. Details: PRIVACY_DATA_FLOW.md.

## 14. Known clinical questions

1. Are the RED and YELLOW lists right? Approve the owner's five additions? (SAFETY_AUDIT §6)
2. How should harm-to-others text be handled? It never triggers today. (SAFETY_AUDIT §7)
3. Are the automatic-YELLOW thresholds (2 and 2) right, and should a game "No" count?
4. Should "That's not what I meant" be available on the full crisis screen, after someone said they are in danger?
5. RED → YELLOW, never GREEN, in a visit; but a reload resets to GREEN. Should elevated state persist?
6. Zags wording (§9) and whether Zags should count toward Human First.
7. Substance-flow wording (§11).
8. Supporter guide "go to them if you can" (§12).
9. Progress counts "Hard moments handled" from flows; since F3, crisis-screen opens are no longer recorded or counted. Appropriate?
10. Age policy: self-attested 18+; since F6 an under-18 answer is remembered on the device (it can only be undone by clearing the browser's data). (Also legal.)
11. "Getting help is a strength" note (`HELP_IS_STRENGTH`, shown in About, My Plan, Connect and the first 30 days checklist). It says seeing a professional "doesn't mean you're 'crazy', broken or weak" and mentions medication ("If medication is suggested, that's a choice you make together with a professional"). The intent is good, but naming "crazy", even to reject it, and saying anything about medication should be worded by a clinician. Approve, reword, or remove those two parts.
12. Turn it into a song (6.16): the prompts, the after-song copy, the replay reflections (Lighter / About the same / Heavier), and whether replaying a hard song helps or hurts.
13. Tech check (6.14): all copy, especially "My AI changed or is gone" ("What you felt was real…") and the boundary text for AI custom instructions. Option 6, "I'm not sure what's real" (`ai_reality`: hearing or seeing things others don't, or feeling an AI chose them for a special mission), is **not built**: it needs your wording for the professional-help message before it ships.
14. Faith & hope (6.15, opt-in, needs a chaplain or clinician): the six KJV passages (some are the well-known part of the verse only, e.g. Psalm 30:5 without "his anger endureth but a moment"), the Pray screen, and whether any religious content during distress should be offered at all for people who chose "Bible".

## 15. Known technical questions

- What the web host logs (query strings, IPs), and whether `localStorage` is included in device backups.
- `localStorage` is not encrypted.
