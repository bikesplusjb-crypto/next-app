Grieving someone who died by suicide

Losing someone to suicide is a different kind of grief. Shock, guilt, anger and questions with no answers are all common.

  1. The "what ifs" are common.

    Many people go over and over what they could have done. It doesn't mean it was your fault.

  2. You decide what you share.

    "They died by suicide. I don't want to talk about the details, but I wanted you to know."

  3. Find people who understand.

    Support groups for people who lost someone to suicide meet in person and online. 211 can help you find one.

Grief like this can bring dark thoughts of your own. If it does, call or text 988 and say you lost someone to suicide.
