Feeling lonely tonight

Lonely is a real kind of hurt. A small step toward people can help.

  1. Send one small message.

    It doesn't have to be deep. "Thinking of you. How's your week?" counts.

  2. Be around people, no talking needed.

    A store, a café, a library, a park. Being near people can help.

  3. Talk to someone tonight.

    If it's more than lonely, you can call or text 988, any time. It's for anyone who's struggling.
