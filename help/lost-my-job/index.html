Lost my job: what to do this week

Losing a job can hit your money, your routine and how you see yourself all at once. It isn't the whole story of you.

  1. Apply for unemployment soon.

    Use your state's official .gov site. Waiting can cost you weeks. 211 can help if you get stuck.

  2. Ask about your last paycheck and insurance.

    "When does my last paycheck come, is unused vacation paid out, and what happens with my health insurance?"

  3. Keep a little routine, and tell one person.

    Up at the same time, outside once a day. "I lost my job. I could use someone to talk to this week."

If losing your job ever makes you think about not being here, call or text 988, any time.
