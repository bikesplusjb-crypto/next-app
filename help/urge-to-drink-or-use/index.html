In crisis or thinking about suicide? Call 988 or text 988 (US), any time. In danger right now? Call 911.

Urge to drink or use right now

An urge rises, peaks and passes. You don't have to decide anything right now.

  1. Wait 15 minutes first.

    Set a timer. Tell yourself you'll decide after it goes off.

  2. Change where you are.

    Step outside, go to another room, or get away from what's in front of you.

  3. Reach someone.

    Text or call someone who supports you. You don't have to explain it all.

If you've already been drinking or using: don't drive, drink water, and stay near someone. If someone can't wake up or isn't breathing, call 911.
