Grief tonight: when you miss them

Grief comes and goes. There's no right way to do this.

  1. Let it be what it is.

    Sad, numb, angry, foggy: all of it is normal in grief.

  2. Do something with the missing.

    Look at a photo, hold something of theirs, or write them a few lines.

  3. Reach one person.

    "Today's a hard day. I'm thinking about them."

If you lost someone to suicide, you're not alone. You can call or text 988 to talk about it, any time.
